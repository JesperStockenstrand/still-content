Answers to common questions, and a way to reach us if you need more help.

Common questions

I got a new phone. How do I transfer my data?

Go to Settings → Transfer to new device. This generates a QR code containing your encryption key. On your new phone, install Still and scan the QR code during setup. Then export your data from the old phone (Settings → Export data) and import it on the new one.

I forgot to transfer before wiping my old phone. Is my data gone?

If you had previously exported a backup (.still file) and saved it somewhere — Files, iCloud, Google Drive — you can recover your journal entries if you still have the encryption key from the old device. Without the key, encrypted entries cannot be recovered. This is by design: no one, including us, can read your data.

How do I back up my data?

Go to Settings → Export data. This saves an encrypted .still file that you can store anywhere — iCloud Drive, Google Drive, email to yourself, or a local folder. The file is encrypted and unreadable without your device key.

I reset my streak by accident. Can I undo it?

Once a streak is reset, it cannot be undone. Your original journey start date is always preserved, so your total days are not lost — only the current streak resets. You can view your journey start date on the home screen.

Notifications aren't arriving. What should I check?

Make sure notifications are enabled in Still (Settings → Notifications) and that iOS/Android has granted notification permission to Still (check your device's system Settings → Still → Notifications). If notifications are enabled in both places and still not arriving, try toggling them off and on again in the app.

The app asks for camera permission. Why?

Camera access is used only for the QR code scanner in the device transfer flow (Settings → Transfer to new device). It is not used for anything else and is not required to use the rest of the app.

Can Still see my journal entries?

No. Journal entries are encrypted on your device before being saved, using a key that never leaves your hardware secure enclave. There is no server, no account, and no mechanism by which anyone other than you can read what you write.

How do I delete all my data?

Go to Settings → Clear all data. This deletes all entries, check-ins, streak data, and regenerates your encryption key. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.
